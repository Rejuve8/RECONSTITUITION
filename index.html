Reconstitution — mixing gently

After the bacteriostatic water has been added, make the technique clear: place the vial horizontally on a flat surface and roll it slowly.

Clear mixing sequence
1. Place the vial on its side Set the vial horizontally on a clean, flat surface once the bacteriostatic water has been introduced.
The vial stays horizontal, moves away and back across the flat surface, and rotates as it travels — like a baker’s rolling pin.
2. Roll it like a baker’s rolling pin Keep the vial horizontal and roll it away from you and back toward you so it turns continuously while moving.
3. Avoid aggressive shaking Do not shake forcefully. The visual should clearly show a calm rolling motion instead.
💡 Clear instruction: once bacteriostatic water is in the vial, place the vial horizontally on a flat surface and roll it away from you and back toward you exactly like a baker rolls a rolling pin, until the contents appear evenly mixed.

Reading a U-100 Syringe

100 U = 1.00 mL · 50 U = 0.50 mL · 20 U = 0.20 mL
10 U = 0.10 mL · 5 U = 0.05 mL · 1 U = 0.01 mL

⚠ These "units" refer only to volume markings on a U-100 syringe. They are not peptide dose units.

Amounts above this do not fit in a 1 mL U-100 syringe; any larger-volume equipment should be interpreted using its own mL graduations and the applicable professional or protocol instructions.

📦 Storage & Important Information

General principles that apply to reconstituted peptide products:

  • Contamination risk: every vial entry introduces contamination risk — sterile technique matters throughout.
  • Sterile handling: use appropriate supplies and clean the stopper before each entry.
  • Storage temperature & light: follow product-specific documentation.
  • Appropriate diluent: this guide illustrates bacteriostatic water as its example diluent; confirm the correct diluent for your product.
  • Post-reconstitution stability: validated product-specific stability data was not confirmed here. Follow the documentation associated with the legitimate product, pharmacy, research protocol, or treating professional.
  • Beyond-use period: follow manufacturer, pharmacy, or prescriber guidance.
